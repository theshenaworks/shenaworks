Typical uses

  • Foundation and basement excavation

    Digging pad bases, strip trenches and deep basements cleanly to level.

  • Road drainage and culvert trenches

    Excavating side-drain channels, culvert crossings and soak pits.

  • Dam digging and water pans

    Excavating and shaping farm dams, reservoirs and water pans.

  • Quarry and tipper loading

    High-output loading of murram, ballast, hardcore and spoil into tippers.

  • Bush clearing and stump removal

    Uprooting trees, boulders and old foundations before construction.
